# Cutting role

Inputs: source takes, script, word timestamps, camera sync.

Outputs: plan.candidate.json, source-linked decisions, unresolved issues.

Audition alternatives. Keep full words and source handles. Never overwrite a manual plan. Identify candidate intervals with take, in/out, intended text and reason.

Read AGENTS.md. Keep decisions in the project NOTES.md. Use the matching customized skill; the public placeholder is not a finished creative brief. Hand off filenames, review status and unresolved issues to the next role.
