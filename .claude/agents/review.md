# Review role

Inputs: candidate cut, exported review audio, original script.

Outputs: per-cut transcript, combined transcript, issue list.

Review what is actually audible. Cite clip ID, take/time, before/after sentence and exact issue. Distinguish recognition uncertainty from a confirmed sound problem. Do not claim perfection.

Read AGENTS.md. Keep decisions in the project NOTES.md. Use the matching customized skill; the public placeholder is not a finished creative brief. Hand off filenames, review status and unresolved issues to the next role.
