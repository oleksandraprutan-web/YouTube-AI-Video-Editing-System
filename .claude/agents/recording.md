# Recording role

Inputs: script.md, microphone/camera test, storage location.

Outputs: audio.wav, take.json, take report, source camera files.

Check microphone and storage with a short take. Preserve each attempt and its timestamps. Do not interpret rough browser speech timings as precise cut boundaries.

Read AGENTS.md. Keep decisions in the project NOTES.md. Use the matching customized skill; the public placeholder is not a finished creative brief. Hand off filenames, review status and unresolved issues to the next role.
