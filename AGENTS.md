# Public workflow agent instructions

Read README.md and docs/workflow.md before working on a video. Choose the relevant role in agents/. The files in skills/ are unconfigured placeholders; do not pretend they contain a finished style or production policy. Ask the project owner for missing creative choices or use explicitly documented project defaults.

Keep working media and private customization in .local/ or an external project folder. Track decisions and approvals in that project's NOTES.md. Read the current project and plan again before proposing edits. Keep source files unchanged and every cut traceable to a source take and interval. Do not replace editable clips with a flattened review file. No changes during a manual-editing handoff.

A candidate cut and an ASR report are evidence for review, not final approval. Audition the output in context, preserve full words and natural breaths, and check for repeated source intervals. Do not invent speech to repair a transcript. If the source is unclear, flag its exact take/time for a pickup or review.

Get explicit approval of the cut before design, of boards before motion, and of the final video before delivery/publication. Preserve approved audio thereafter. Do not add music, effects, zooms, or background removal unless requested. Publication and external messaging require the project owner's authorization. This repository contains no API credentials and no private skill pack.
