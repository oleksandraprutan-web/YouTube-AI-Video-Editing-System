Eyeline

Teleprompter that follows your voice

Put directions in [square brackets]. They show on screen in amber, and the prompter won't wait for you to say them. A direction that says Chapter becomes a chapter you can jump to. Markdown is cleaned up for the prompter automatically, and tables with a Voiceover column keep only the voiceover.
